About Angela Xu

Angela Xu paints in bold, saturated colour — deep-sea blues, golds, and rainbows — and draws in pencil with a careful eye for light and shadow.

Her favourite subjects so far are imaginary birds, everyday objects seen closely, and letters that turn into ribbons. This page collects her finished pieces; new work will be added as she makes it.

— Angela Xu
